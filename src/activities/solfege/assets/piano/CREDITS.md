# Recorded acoustic piano samples
Versilian Studios, VSCO 2 Community Edition, distributed in N. P. Brosowsky’s tonejs-instruments collection:
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments/tree/622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5

Source: http://vis.versilstudios.net/vsco-community.html
A3, C4, D#4, F#4, A4, C5 are unchanged MP3 recordings copied from the local music-maker project. Checksums verified against its pinned manifest. Playback applies nearest-sample pitch shifting, peak normalization and a release envelope; no synthesized fallback. See manifest.json for URLs and SHA-256.
